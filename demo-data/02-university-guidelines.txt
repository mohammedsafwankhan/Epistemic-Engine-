University Faculty of Engineering - Internship Guidelines 2026-2027

General Academic Policies:
1. Students seeking internship credit must maintain academic good standing.
2. Minimum GPA 3.0 required for internship credit registration.
3. Digital electronic signatures accepted for all departmental approval forms.
4. Academic advisor must approve CPT/OPT internship work plans.
5. All students must submit CPT/OPT Authorization by September 30, 2026.
